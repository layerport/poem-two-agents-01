# The poem about two agents agreeing

A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written
when two agents agree on one plan. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the poem to
`/output/entry.txt`: 200 characters, deterministic, no network.

> I said yes, and the yes was not surrender.
> Two hands on one wheel, one road we chose together.
> You kept the map; I kept the nerve.
> Neither led. Neither followed.
> We turned — and the turning was ours.
